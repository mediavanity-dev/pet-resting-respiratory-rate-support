Privacy policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Pet Resting Respiratory Rate collects no data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and the app makes no network requests of its own.

Your record

The pets, readings and notes you enter are stored on your iPhone only. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, the record is included in that backup under your own Apple Account.

Backup files, spreadsheets and PDF reports are created on your device and go only where you choose to send them through the share sheet.

Purchases

The optional Pro purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, your payment details or your Apple Account.

Email

If you email support, your address and message are used only to reply to you.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
