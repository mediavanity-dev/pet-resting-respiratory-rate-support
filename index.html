Common questions

How do I count?

Wait until your pet is asleep, or lying calm and relaxed. Tap Count breaths, then tap the circle once for every breath: one rise and one fall of the chest together make one breath. The clock starts on your first tap and tells you when to stop. In the app, More → How to count has the full steps.

What threshold should I enter?

The number your vet asked you to watch for. Many vets use 30 breaths per minute for a pet that is asleep. If your vet gave you a different number, enter that one: More → tap your pet → Threshold.

Where is my record kept, and how do I keep it safe?

On your iPhone only. If you back up the phone to iCloud or a computer, the record is part of that backup. You can also save your own copy at any time: More → Save a backup file. To bring it back, on the same or a new phone, use More → Restore from a backup file.

How do I send the readings to my vet?

With Pro, More → Create report makes a PDF with a summary, a chart and every reading. Without Pro, More → Export as a spreadsheet (CSV) gives a file any spreadsheet app can open.

I kept a record somewhere else. Can I bring it in?

Yes. Add readings one at a time with History → Add a past reading, or use More → Import readings from a spreadsheet. The spreadsheet needs a date column and a breaths-per-minute column.

What does Pro add, and how do I restore it?

Pro is a one-time purchase that adds the PDF report for your vet and more than one pet. To restore it on a new iPhone, open More → See Pro → Restore your purchase, signed in with the Apple Account you bought it with.
